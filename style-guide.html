Dollz style guide

Pink sticker-style UI around a pixel-art doll. Every value comes from tokens.css; every component lives in shared.css.

Principles Tokens Layout Components Doll rules

Principles

  • Sticker look: --border-width ink borders and hard, un-blurred shadows (--shadow-hard). No soft shadows, no gradients.
  • Pink on pink. Panels are a lighter pink, the page is a mid pink, the accent is the deepest pink. Ink (--color-ink) is the only dark color.
  • Pixel display font for headings, labels and buttons. Friendly sans for everything else.
  • No raw values in shared.css. A new value goes into tokens.css first.
  • The only inline styles are data-driven custom properties: --swatch on each swatch button, and --doll-outline on the root (both set by the script).
  • Decorative motion (the cursor sparkles and the flashing letters in the page title) is switched off for people who ask for reduced motion.

Tokens

Values below are read live from tokens.css.

Layout

App grid (.app)

Two equal columns at every viewport width (--layout-columns: 2), centered, --layout-max-width (900px). The title spans both columns and is centered. Row 2 holds the stage panel and the options panel, both 80vh tall (80dvh on phones, where 100vh counts the area under the browser bar): the stage panel sets the height and the options panel stretches to match and scrolls inside it. The Save button sits under the stage panel and the hit counter beside it, so both stay in view on screens at least about 600px tall.

<main class="app">
  <h1 class="app__title">…</h1>
  <div class="panel panel--stage"><canvas class="stage__canvas"></canvas></div>
  <section class="panel app__controls">…</section>
  <button class="btn app__export">Save as PNG</button>
  <div class="hits">…</div>
</main>

Page title (.app__title, .app__title-letter)

˚₊‧꒰ა Dollz ໒꒱ ‧₊˚

<h1 class="app__title">˚₊‧꒰ა
  <span class="app__title-letter">D</span>…<span class="app__title-letter">z</span>
໒꒱ ‧₊˚</h1>
  • Centered, in the display font at --font-size-xl. The browser tab and this guide just say "Dollz"; the decoration is only in the page title.
  • The marks around the name come from your system's fallback fonts (Pixelify Sans has no glyphs for them), so they look different on each device.
  • Each letter of "Dollz" is its own span. They flash to --color-accent one at a time, then back one at a time, in a 4.2s loop (title-flash). The colors jump with no fades (steps(1, end)), each letter hops up 3px when it turns on, wobbles 1px on two frames while it's lit and drops 2px when it turns off, and the delays (0, 0.4, 0.65, 1.2, 1.35s) are uneven on purpose, so it stutters like an old animated GIF. The marks stay still.
  • Not animated under prefers-reduced-motion.

Panel (.panel, .panel--stage)

A panel: --color-bg-panel fill, ink border, --shadow-hard, --panel-padding.
The stage variant uses --color-bg-stage (and is 80vh tall in the app).
  • .app__controls overrides --color-bg-panel to white locally, so chips and swatch rings inside it follow. Its own bottom padding is 0: the room under the last control is padding-bottom: var(--panel-padding) on .tabpanel, because some browsers (Safari) leave a scroller's bottom padding out of the scrollable area.
  • .stage__canvas fills the stage panel and fits the doll inside it with object-fit: contain (letterboxed, never stretched or cropped), using image-rendering: pixelated. The panel is 80vh; --stage-ratio (7 / 12) is kept as a reference but the CSS no longer uses it, because the doll's own proportions decide the fit.
  • .panel--stage is position: relative so the randomize button can sit in its top-right corner.

Background hearts (body::before)

A fixed layer behind everything: a 3×3 pixel heart (X.X / XXX / .X.) used as a mask over --color-bg-hearts, tiled at --heart-tile (--heart-pixel × 7). Change the heart's color or size by changing tokens; the shape lives in --bg-heart-shape. Needs the -webkit-mask prefix for Safari before 15.4.

Components

Button (.btn)

<button class="btn" type="button">Save as PNG</button>
  • Fill --color-accent, text --color-accent-text, display font, --shadow-hard-sm.
  • Hover lifts it and grows the shadow to --shadow-hard. Active presses it flat. Both are disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Focus: dashed ink outline (shared with chip, swatch, slider and tab).

Randomize (.randomize, .dice)

<button class="randomize" id="randomize" type="button">
  <svg class="dice" aria-hidden="true">…rects…</svg>
  <span class="randomize__label">randomize</span>
</button>
  • One button: the dice icon and the word are a single click target. It is absolutely positioned in the top-right corner of the stage panel.
  • The icon is a 9 × 14 pixel grid drawn as SVG rects (shape-rendering: crispEdges), each pixel --dice-pixel wide. Its colors are tokens: outline --color-dice-outline (= the border ink), fill --color-dice-fill, pips --color-dice-pip.
  • Hidden for now. In the app the button carries the hidden attribute, and .randomize[hidden] { display: none } makes it stick (the attribute alone loses to the button's display: flex). Delete the attribute to bring it back; all of the code is still there. The demo above is always shown.
  • Hover turns the label and the dice outline to --color-accent. Pressing moves only the dice down by --border-width; the label stays put. The press shift is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

  • Decoration only: it is not a real counter. The number is always 6980085 (seven boxes) and nothing is stored or counted.
  • It sits in grid column 2 under the options panel, beside Save as PNG. Each digit is a box --hit-digit-width wide, ink fill, --color-pink-100 digit.

Cursor sparkles (.sparkles)

  • One full-window canvas at --z-overlay with pointer-events: none, so it never takes a click. It is in the app, not on this page: move the mouse over index.html to see it.
  • Pink pixel sparkles and hearts (about one in three) trail the mouse on a 3px grid, in --color-pink-300, -500 and -600 (white would vanish on the white panel).
  • Sparkles start as a five-pixel plus, shrink to a three-pixel plus and then a dot, flicker between pinks and sink. Hearts start as a five-pixel heart, shrink to the three-pixel heart used in the page background and then a dot, hold one pink and float up.
  • Mouse and pen only (no touch), at most 90 alive at once, and the animation loop runs only while something is on screen. Not drawn at all under prefers-reduced-motion.

Cursors (--cursor-default, --cursor-pointer)

  • Two pixel-art cursors in Bubblegum pink (#FF8FC0, the Bubblegum skin tone) with a black outline. The colors are written inside the images, since a CSS variable can't be used there.
  • Arrow: 10×16 pixels drawn at 2× (20×32px), tip at 0 0. It is set on html, so everything inherits it.
  • Pointing hand: 13×16 pixels at 2× (26×32px), fingertip at 9 1. It is set on every clickable thing: buttons, chips, swatches, tabs, the dice and the slider.
  • Each falls back to the browser's own arrow or hand if the image can't load. Move the mouse over this page to see them.

Tabs (.tabs, .tab, .tabpanel)

<div class="tabs" role="tablist">
  <button class="tab" role="tab" aria-selected="true">Figure</button>
  <button class="tab" role="tab" aria-selected="false">Hair</button>
</div>
<div class="tabpanel" role="tabpanel">…controls…</div>
  • Order: Figure, Hair, Tops, Bottoms, Dress, Accessories, Presets (Dress and Accessories are empty for now). Presets is last so the page still opens on Figure; it holds one control (see Doll rules, Presets).
  • Flush to the top, left and right of the options panel, as if the panel's padding weren't there: a negative margin equal to --panel-padding pulls it out, and the sticky top takes the same negative value (a sticky offset is measured from inside the padding, so top: 0 would leave a gap). The pale underline therefore runs edge to edge.
  • The panel's inset lives on the tabs, not on the nav: every tab gets --panel-padding extra on top, the first tab on its left and the last tab on its right. The labels line up with the controls below, and the spacing between tabs is unchanged.
  • Sticky at the top of the panel (--z-sticky) and scrolls sideways when the tabs don't fit. Its scrollbar is never shown (scrollbar-width: none and ::-webkit-scrollbar { display: none }); scroll by trackpad, touch or drag.
  • Selected state is the aria-selected="true" attribute: ink text and an accent underline. There is no separate "active" class.
  • Arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs; roving tabindex.

Control (.control)

Skin tone Sand 
<div class="control">
  <div class="control__head">
    <span class="control__label">Skin tone</span>
    <span class="control__value">Sand</span>
  </div>
  <!-- one of: swatch-group, chip-group, or input.slider -->
</div>

Every option group is a control: a label row (name left, current value right in muted text) over one option set. All controls are built by one function in index.html, so the markup is identical everywhere.

Conditional controls. A control can hide itself with showIf (a function). dependsOn (a setting name, or a list) names the settings that can change it, so choosing one rebuilds the panel, keeping its scroll position and the focused option. Belt color and Shoe color use this: Belt color shows only for pants that have a belt (the Flares' belt setting) and Shoe color only for pants that come with shoes (the Flares' shoes), so neither shows for High-waist or None. So do the Tops rows: Under shirt color (only the Puffer), Design (only the Graphic) and the accent row (only tops that have one, hidden when the Graphic's design is the Monkey or None).

Labels that change with the top. A control's label can be a function. A top can rename its color rows with labels: Layered and Puffer call the main color "Jacket color", Layered calls the accent "Undershirt color", the Puffer's third row is "Under shirt color", and the Graphic's accent is "Design color". The rows always run in this order: Top, Under shirt color, Top color, Design, accent.

Swatch (.swatch-group, .swatch)

  • Round, ink-bordered color option; size --swatch-size (smaller below 480px).
  • Color comes from the --swatch custom property, set inline from the preset data. "Match outline" options set it to var(--doll-outline), so they track the live skin.
  • Selected: aria-checked="true" adds a two-ring halo (panel color, then ink). Hover lifts it slightly.
  • Always give it an aria-label and a title; there is no visible text.
  • An option can carry hidden: true: it stays in the list (so anything that names it still resolves) but is not drawn and is never rolled by Randomize. The nine hidden top colors are Periwinkle, Blush, Lilac, Orchid, Fuchsia, Indigo, Cornflower, Petal and Lemon.
  • The top colors are ordered monochrome first (Black, Charcoal, Gray, White), then the rainbow from red to the pinks: Red, Orange, Yellow, Butter, Cream, Green, Mint, Emerald, Teal, Sky, Blue, Steel, Navy, Purple, Lavender, Hot Pink, Pink. The top color Black is a very dark blue-black (#232B47), so the outline (value × 0.8) stays distinguishable from the fill.

Chip (.chip-group, .chip)

<div class="chip-group" role="radiogroup">
  <button class="chip" role="radio" aria-checked="true">Lashes</button>
  <button class="chip" role="radio" aria-checked="false">Cat's eye</button>
</div>
  • Pill-shaped text option for choices that aren't colors (figure, eye style, hair style, top, design, bottom, preset). Selected is filled with --color-accent. Chips are not tabs: tabs are the underlined nav, chips are options inside a panel.
  • Every chip row is one line that scrolls sideways (trackpad, touch, Shift + wheel, or Tab) and never wraps; chips don't shrink. The scrollbar is never shown. The second demo is the Top row in a narrow panel. Color swatches still wrap.
  • The chosen chip is scrolled into view after a pick, a Randomize or a tab switch, and the row keeps its position when the panel rebuilds.
  • On the Presets tab the same chips set many picks at once instead of one. A preset chip is selected only while every pick in that preset is still the current pick, so changing any pick elsewhere deselects it. No extra CSS.
  • Vertical padding (--ring-gap + --border-width) leaves room for the focus outline, which a scroll container would otherwise clip.

Slider (.slider)

Pill track (--slider-track-height) with a round accent thumb (--slider-thumb-size), styled for both WebKit and Firefox. The value readout in the label row is "NN%".

Empty state (.empty-state)

Nothing to customize here yet.

Shown in any tab that has no controls. Dashed muted border, centered muted text.

Scrollbars

Only the options panel (.app__controls, always visible) shows a scrollbar: a pink thumb on a stage-pink track, --scrollbar-size wide. WebKit/Blink use ::-webkit-scrollbar; Firefox gets scrollbar-color inside @supports not selector(::-webkit-scrollbar) because Chrome ignores the webkit styling whenever scrollbar-color is set. The tab bar and every chip row scroll sideways with their scrollbars always hidden.

Doll rules

Stage and export

  • Stage is 84×144 logical pixels (7:12). The figure sits 6 rows from the top (offset.y) so tall hair has room above the head. All figures, hair styles and clothing share this coordinate space.
  • The preview is fitted inside the stage panel (letterboxed) and drawn at a whole-number scale, sized by whichever of the panel's width or height runs out first, so every doll pixel is the same size. Export is a transparent PNG at 8× (672×1152), named doll_YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS.png in the viewer's local time, so every save gets its own name.

Outline color

Outline = skin color with its HSV value multiplied by a factor. The default factor is 0.9. A skin tone can override it with outlineFactor, or pin an exact outline.

Skin toneOutline rule
Most tones (Porcelain, Ivory, Peach, fun colors, Light Gray, White)Value × 0.9 (default)

Bronze, Mocha, EspressoValue × 0.65
Black (skin #333333)Pinned to #000000

Tops

  • Tops are overlays like hair: grids in figure coordinates, drawn over the figure, pants and shoes and under the hair (hair stays in front). The order in the list is the order of the chips in the Top row, with None last: T-shirt, Graphic, Ombre, Puffer, Layered, Tube Top, Wrap Top, Triangle, Cami, Lined Jacket, None.
  • The T-shirt is the original artwork. Every other top came from a pixel file with the pink nose marker, whose canvas sat 4 pixels right and 5 down from the figure (marker (29,30)-(30,30), nose (25,25)-(26,25)), so figure = file − (4, 5). Pink pixels other than the two marker pixels are part of the garment.
  • Every top is fully opaque (TOP_OPACITY = 1, one setting; set it below 1 and tops become see-through again, including in the saved PNG). Edge pixels where the doll's painted-in bra or outline peeked past a top were covered with outline-tone pixels, top by top.
  • Codes: F fill, X outline (the fill's value × 0.8), A accent, M lighter fill (the Layered jacket's streaks), C darker accent (the Layered undershirt's folds), u and v under shirt and its lighter streak (Puffer), 6 and 7 under shirt shades (Lined Jacket), 1 2 3 4 5 even blends from fill to accent in sixths (Ombre), and the Graphic monkey's fixed colors (c d f g h plus black L).
  • Picking a top sets its own start colors: black for the main color of every top except the Lined Jacket (a Yellow jacket); two-color tops add white (the Ombre's accent, the Layered's undershirt, the Puffer's under shirt, the Graphic's design color), and the Lined Jacket adds a Black lining and a White undershirt.
  • Ombre: the artwork's seven color bands, top to bottom, are seven steps of one fade: Top color at the top, then five even blends, then Accent color at the bottom. Only the band shapes come from the artwork, never its colors.
  • Graphic: the chest picture is a swappable design: Monkey (the artwork's own fixed colors, not recolorable), or the shared Peace, Heart and Smiley (drawn in the Design color) or None. The Design row only shows on the Graphic, and the Design color row hides for the Monkey and for None. The shared designs are positioned on this top's chest; another top would need them re-placed.
  • Lined Jacket: from a pixel file whose nose marker sat at cells (26, 4)-(27, 4) on a grid of 15.72 file pixels per art pixel (every cell one color), so figure = file cell − (1, −21). Three colors: Under shirt color (the undershirt, with two shades derived as the color's value × 0.8 and × 0.64, codes 6 and 7), Jacket color (F, with the pale streaks M derived lighter and the file's tan, the shaded rim of the sleeves, as X, the fill's value × 0.8) and Lining color (A, the Accent color row relabeled: only the file's black, the edge along the open front and hem, including two dark-purple collar pixels read as the same black). The white gaps between the sleeve and the body are empty cells, so the doll shows through.

Bottoms

  • Pants are overlays like tops: drawn over the figure, under the shoes and the shirt. Two tones: Q fill and R outline, the fill's value × PANTS_OUTLINE_FACTOR (0.85, a soft step). The Bottom color list is shared by every pants style.
  • The row is Flares, High-waist, Lined Shorts, None (None last; Randomize never picks it).
  • Picking pants can set its own start colors, as tops do. Only the Lined Shorts does (see below); Flares and High-waist keep the current picks.
  • High-waist came from a pixel file drawn on the whole 84×144 stage with the nose marker at (44, 31)-(45, 31), so figure = file − (19, 6). The file's own two blues were deliberately not added as colors: its cyan is the fill and its dark blue the outline, so both follow the Bottom color, and the strong contrast of the artwork becomes the same gentle 0.85 step as the Flares.
  • Flares also has a belt (code G, the gaps enclosed by the waistband, filled by the Belt color) and its own shoes, drawn right after the pants.
  • Lined Shorts came from a pixel file whose nose marker sat at cells (26, 4)-(27, 4) on a grid of 15.72 file pixels per art pixel, so figure = file cell − (1, −21). Bottom color is the shorts (Q fill, R outline, 8 lighter streaks, derived like the hair highlight) and the wide waistband is the lining: the Belt color row, relabeled Lining color (G, with 9 its lighter streaks). Hair styles

  • "Bald" is the cap painted into the figure (no overlay) and is first in the row. Every other style is an overlay drawn on top of that cap; the cap is never hidden. The order below is the order of the chips, with Short last.
  • Overlays are grids in figure coordinates with three tones: D = shadow, H = base, V = highlight. Every style uses all three except Short, which uses only H and V. When a reference has three colors, the darkest is the shadow, the middle the base and the lightest the highlight; all three follow the Hair color you pick.
  • Draw order, back to front: the figure, pants, shoes, shirt, then hair. Hair always sits in front of everything.
  • No style covers the eye pixels (iris, shine, white) of either eye: cells over them are cleared (the right eye's white at (31, 22) in Short and Wavy, and the left iris at (21, 21) in Long, which a screenshot shows in a green close to the hair).
  • Most styles are clean pixel-art references. The screenshot's grid is fitted from where its colors change, every cell is read and rebuilt to check it (0 mismatches), and near-duplicate shades are folded into the three tones. With a pink nose marker the offset is exact (marker = the nose at (25, 25)-(26, 25)); when a screenshot shows the whole face instead, the face is lined up with ours by matching its lip and lash pixels (all of them matched), and no marker is needed. The whole-face screenshots (Long, Side Pony, Low Pigs) put the hair one pixel higher than the marker-based placement, so their top-left y is 4.
  • Rumi is a long braid over the doll's right shoulder, so its top-left y is 0 (the hair itself starts on the stage's first figure row) and it is the widest and tallest style. Its screenshot's lighter-purple brows belong to the figure and are not in the overlay. Eight of the figure's cap pixels by the left temple are skin in that screenshot but stay hair-colored here, because the cap is never hidden.
  • Only Straight Bob and Short Layers are still traced from small, blurry screenshots (about 3.5 screenshot pixels per art pixel), so they are resamples, with cells beside the jaw and neck filled with the shadow tone.

Presets

  • A preset is a whole look saved as picks: an id, a name and a state of the same keys and ids the controls use (PRESETS in index.html). A key left out keeps the current pick.
  • The Presets tab has one control, Preset, a chip row with no state key of its own. Picking a chip copies the preset into the state, rebuilds the panel and redraws the doll. A chip is selected only while every pick in it is still current.
  • Presets only use swatches and styles that already exist; they add no colors. Randomize skips the Preset control, and the page still loads on the default look, not on a preset.

Randomize rules

  • The Randomize button is hidden for now (see Components). These are the rules for when it's back.
  • It rolls every setting: skin, eyes, eyeshadow and its opacity, lips and their opacity, undies, hair style and color, brow color, top, its colors, design and design color, bottoms and their color, belt color, shoe color and trim color. Hidden swatches are never rolled. The Preset control is skipped.
